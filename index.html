The Program

More than an internship. An opportunity to grow.

At IRMARFER, we believe that young talent brings new perspectives, fresh ideas and the ambition to shape the future.

Through our Young Talent Program, we welcome students and recent graduates who are eager to learn, develop their skills and gain valuable professional experience.

Whether you are looking for a curricular internship, a professional internship or your first opportunity to enter the job market, IRMARFER could be the place to take your next step.

Curricular

For students who need practical experience as part of their course.

Extracurricular

For students who want experience outside their course plan.

Professional

For recent graduates who want their first professional experience.

Summer

For students who want experience during the summer break.
